Privacy Policy

Graphium for macOS. Last updated November 15, 2026.

The short version

Graphium collects no data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash upload, and it never sends your campaigns anywhere. Your maps, tokens, notes and sounds stay in files on your Mac that you own.

Your campaigns

A campaign is a file you choose where to save. Graphium reads and writes it on your Mac only. Sound folders you add are read where they are and never uploaded.

When Graphium uses the network

Graphium contacts another computer only when you ask it to:

  • Board pack downloads. If a board pack you import lists files on the web, Graphium names those sites and asks first. It downloads the files only if you choose Download. Those sites see an ordinary web request from your Mac, as they would from a browser. Graphium's developer receives nothing.
  • AI Tools. Off until you choose a campaign folder and turn them on in Settings > AI Tools. An AI app you run yourself, such as Claude or Codex, then talks to Graphium through a helper on your Mac. Graphium itself sends nothing over the network, but the AI app sees the maps it reads and edits and may send them to its own service, under that company's privacy policy.
  • Links. Help and About links, such as Report a Bug, open in your web browser.

Diagnostics

Graphium keeps logs and crash reports on your Mac, without campaign content. Help > Collect Diagnostics puts them in a folder you can review. Nothing is sent unless you attach it to a bug report yourself. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in macOS settings, Apple handles that under Apple's own privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will say so and show a new date. Questions go to Graphium's issue tracker or SUPPORT_EMAIL.
