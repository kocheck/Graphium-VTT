Graphium Support

Graphium VTT for macOS. A virtual tabletop for the Dungeon Master.

Get help

  • Report a bug or ask for a feature on GitHub Issues. Search open issues first in case it's already there. You need a free GitHub account, and issues are public.

Before you attach anything

Help > Collect Diagnostics puts Graphium's logs and crash reports in a folder you can read first. They leave out campaign content, but a screenshot or a campaign file shows your maps, notes and names. Issues are public, so only attach what you're happy for anyone to see.

Good to know

  • Graphium needs macOS 14 or later.
  • A campaign is a .graphiumcampaign folder you choose where to save. Back it up like any other file. Earlier versions are kept as checkpoints (File > Restore Checkpoint).
  • The World View is the players' window. Open it with Shift-Command-W, then choose Show on Display to put it on a TV or projector.
  • AI Tools (Settings > AI Tools) are off until you choose a campaign folder and turn them on. Graphium must be open for an AI app to edit a map.

Privacy

Graphium collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
